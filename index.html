Forensic Intelligence FAQ

How does VERITAS AI detect deepfakes?

VERITAS AI uses Error Level Analysis (ELA) to identify compression quantization mismatches, 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to detect high-frequency GAN/diffusion checkerboard grids, and a ResNet-18 neural backbone to evaluate facial symmetry and boundaries.

What is the difference between Demo Mode and Production API?

In Demo Mode, client-side Canvas algorithms compute actual Error Level Analysis and Laplacian frequency maps with simulated neural weights. When connected to the backend API, media is processed by live multi-GPU ResNet-18 clusters with full C2PA signature issuance.

Are uploaded files stored or used to train models?

No. VERITAS AI enforces a strict zero-retention policy. Files are processed in volatile memory and purged immediately once the cryptographic hash and audit metrics are calculated.
